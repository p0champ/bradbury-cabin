# Build 058: one short Quest check

Allow at most ten minutes. Keep your existing browser data and backup.
These physical checks are still pending until you try them. Stop at ten
minutes and leave any unfinished check marked pending.

1. **Two minutes — opening and resume.** Check that the opening page says
   Build 058. Close older cabin tabs and tap Sync with Drive once to connect
   this Quest. Enter, turn a page, change one setting, then
   restart the Quest and reopen the browser; check that both return. The room, pointer, intro,
   page position and wrist-watch design should feel as before.
2. **Two minutes — Claude's three.** Ask Claude for the daily three while
   the cabin is open; they should appear within a minute. Ask for a poem
   swap; the story and essay must stay unchanged, and an open story must
   stay on its page. If today's three are not here yet, check the calm note
   and the link to yesterday's three.
3. **Two minutes — finish and thought.** Finish a short poem: the optional
   stars card should offer the next unread piece. Try Speak and say one
   sentence; check whether the words appear before choosing Save. If they
   do not, skip the thought and report it. Reread a finished short piece;
   its final Next should lead straight to Library.
4. **One minute — watch and menus.** With a story open, the watch should
   name it. Check that Move page here and Comfort are easy to reach and
   the corner ✕ leaves. Rewards should show unfinished goals with real
   counts; Shelf and Library should explain what they contain.
5. **One minute — no internet.** Turn Wi-Fi off, turn a page and tap Sync:
   reading must continue with a calm note. Turn Wi-Fi back on and tap Sync
   again. After leaving, check that your place is saved and no ordinary
   test-details button appears.
6. **Two minutes — outside recovery.** In a separate browser or device,
   open the cabin and connect to the same account. After the quiet reopen,
   check your saved place, setting and any saved thought. Keep the original
   Quest copy intact. If you run out of time, leave this marked pending.

During your next seven normal reading days, keep using the same Claude
requests and report any missed arrival or lost change. A computer check
cannot substitute for those seven real days.
