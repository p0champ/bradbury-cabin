# Build 007 — centring and native pinch check

Open [Build 007](https://p0champ.github.io/bradbury-cabin/?v=7) in a fresh Quest Browser tab with hand tracking enabled. Check the header says **BUILD 007**. Crispness already passed; this checks controls. No gesture pass is claimed yet.

1. **Enter and settle.** Lie comfortably and look where you want the page. The first panel must say **Get comfortable**. Menu, practice and page turns must stay inactive. Opening a menu immediately is a failure.
2. **Centre once.** Keep an open hand clearly in view for about half a second, then pinch and release once. The page should be placed there. Moving your head afterwards must leave it still. This confirmation must not also turn a page or open a menu.
3. **Turn deliberately.** While looking toward the page with your hand inside view, right pinch/release goes Next, left goes Back. Repeat about ten times, also lying down. Real turns flash the arrows. The sample has only two pages: past the first/last page, an orange end bar and text indicate the boundary; that direction is dimmed. Count misses where a page really was available.
4. **Check the hold.** Hold either pinch while looking toward the page. The hand ring should appear after a short delay and fill until the menu opens at about 1.4 seconds. Release; nothing else should activate. Only the hand layers should animate.
5. **Try menu buttons.** Point until a button highlights, then pinch and release. Try Paper/Night, swapping hands, Return to reading and Re-centre. Re-centre should deliberately place the reading page in your current view. The menu itself stays still. Press on one button, move onto another, and release: the other button must not activate.
6. **Look away.** While starting a pinch/hold, turn away from the page. Actions should pause and the ring should clear. Returning with fingers still pinched must do nothing. Open the hand, keep it in view briefly, then make a new pinch to resume.
7. **Check tracking and idle hands.** Rest both hands, move one toward/outside the edge of view and bring it back already pinched. Nothing should turn or open. Reopen before trying again. Quest's own palm-facing system gesture must not also operate the cabin. Returning from the system UI needs a fresh open hand.
8. **Report and save.** Exit to the browser and use **Save test details**. If the menu is unresponsive, use Quest's system controls to leave XR first, then save. Send the file with whether centring worked, missed clicks, phantom actions and what your hands/head were doing. Do not approve the gesture gate unless it feels reliable.

The export includes native events received and validated, tracking/view/readiness state, cancellations and actual outcomes. It can show whether Quest emitted a click that the app rejected or no native event arrived. All current automated evidence is simulated on PC; only Bastian can pass the headset gate.
