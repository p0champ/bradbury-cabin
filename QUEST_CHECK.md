# Build 006 — gesture repair check

Build 005 was reported as "registered but doesn't change page". Build 006 changes what counts as a pinch and makes the feedback tell the truth. Open [Build 006](https://p0champ.github.io/bradbury-cabin/?v=6) in a fresh Quest Browser tab with hand tracking on. The header should say BUILD 006.

1. Enter the cabin. If practice appears, right pinch-and-release, then left. Each ticks; after both, it should drop you straight into reading.
2. The sample is only **2 pages**. On page 1 the left-hand (back) icon should look dim. Right pinch-and-release: page 2 appears and the arrows flash.
3. On page 2, right pinch again: the page stays, the right icon shows an **orange end bar** (no arrow flash) and the footer says "Last page". That is a correct, honest no-op.
4. Left pinch: back to page 1. Repeat Next/Back 10 times at a normal, relaxed pace. Count any pinch that did nothing on a page where it should have turned.
5. Quick pinches should **not** show the gold ring. Hold a pinch: the ring appears after about a third of a second and fills; the menu opens at about 1.4 s.
6. Rest your hands loosely, fingers almost touching, for a few seconds, both sitting and lying down. Nothing should happen.
7. Half-close a pinch and linger, then open. Nothing should happen.
8. If anything misfires, open the menu, use **Exit to browser**, then **Save test details** and send me the file. It now lists each pinch attempt and why it did or didn't count.

Tell me: did every intended turn happen, did any accidental turn happen, and does the release feel natural?

# Build 005 — the gesture trial

Bastian passed the crispness gate on Build 003. This trial checks the new controls. Open [Build 005](https://p0champ.github.io/bradbury-cabin/?v=5) in Quest Browser with hand tracking on.

1. Lie down, look where you want to read, and select **Enter the cabin**. On first use you should see practice. Open your fingers once so each hand is ready.
2. Pinch firmly for about a quarter second with your resting right hand, then **release**. Practice should tick **Next**. Your reading page should not advance during practice.
3. Do the same with your left hand for **Back**. Then hold either pinch for about **1.4 seconds**: a circle should fill on that hand and the menu should open. Release; no button should activate by itself.
4. Point at **Return to reading** until it highlights, pinch, then release. If pointing is uncomfortable, **Pointer: hand** switches to a head-directed pointer inside the menu. That option is also in the browser before entering.
5. Read the sample. Right pinch/release goes forward; left goes back. Try a few resting positions wherever the headset still tracks your hand. Looking around should not change these actions.
6. Turn your head a little and hold a pinch for the menu. It should appear in your current view and stay still. Hold again to close it: the reading page should remain where you left it. **Re-centre page** deliberately moves that page in front of you.
7. Try **Forward: right hand** to swap the hands. Check both directions. Try Paper/Night and Text size. Keep an idle hand visible while selecting with the other: the highlight must match the pinching hand.
8. Use **Exit to browser**. Tell me whether Next/Back/Menu feel reliable, whether the hold feels too short or long, and any double turns, wrong selections or poorly tracked resting positions. Use **Save test details** if needed.

Keep palm-facing-you pinches near your face for Quest's system controls. A hold elsewhere opens the cabin menu. **Practise gestures** is always available there. The completed practice and control preferences are cached locally; reading history and Claude are not part of this trial. Actual hand tracking and comfort still require your check.
