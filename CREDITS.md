# Texts and type

- William Blake (1757–1827), **The Sick Rose**, _Songs of Experience_ (1794). Original English, public domain. Source: [Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Songs_of_Experience/The_Sick_Rose). Line breaks retained; historical capitalization retained in the test text.
- O. Henry (William Sydney Porter, 1862–1910), **The Gift of the Magi** (1905; collected in _The Four Million_, 1906). First two paragraphs only. Original English, public domain. Source: [Project Gutenberg #2776](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2776). Only the underlying public-domain text is reproduced, without Gutenberg branding or editorial content.
- **Literata**, Google Fonts / TypeTogether. Bundled unmodified variable font under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full copyright and licence: [assets/Literata-OFL.txt](assets/Literata-OFL.txt). [Official source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/literata). UI uses the device's installed sans-serif; those font files are not redistributed. Font rasterization and antialiasing are provided by the browser; there is no promise of a particular hinting mode on Quest.
- No sound, photographs, or third-party JavaScript libraries are included in Phase 0.

Both authors died more than 70 years ago. Modern translations or edited versions may have different rights; these samples use the original English texts.

- The skip icons, hold rings and Build 008 paper-and-gold welcome leaves are original procedural Canvas artwork. No third-party images, 3D models or new assets are loaded for the welcome.

- Build 009's hand-entry orbit and welcome chime are original procedural artwork/audio. The chime uses three synthesised sine tones, with no recordings or third-party sound assets.

- The Build 009 hand labels are also original Canvas drawing.
