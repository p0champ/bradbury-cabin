# Your Bradbury Cabin

Open your usual cabin bookmark and enter. Your page stays where you placed it;
your last reading place and settings return when you reopen.

Close any older cabin tabs first. The first time you open Build 058 on the
Quest, tap **Sync with Drive** on
the opening page and choose your usual Google account. This connects that
Quest's saved places and settings to the complete outside backup.

## Each day

Ask Claude **“give me the daily three”**, just as before. Keep the cabin open:
the checked poem, story and essay should appear within about a minute while
connected. For a replacement, ask **“swap the poem”** (or story or essay).
The other two stay the same. Nothing chooses readings without your request.

Use **Today** for your three, **Shelf** for earlier days, and **Library** for
every available work, including starters and earned gifts. Finish a piece
with the final confirmation; turning its last page alone does not mark it read.

Stars and thoughts are optional. Choose **Save** only when you approve the
thought. An unfinished thought can return marked **unsaved**; it is not an
approved thought. If speaking does not work on your Quest, keep reading and
tell me what happened before any replacement is built.

Rewards show your real progress. One missed or half-read day per Monday–Sunday
week protects your streak without adding a reading day. Earned gifts stay yours.

## Saving

Build 058 keeps your usual tap-to-Sync connection; it does not use Cloudflare.
Reading, settings, thoughts and drafts save on this device. Tap **Sync with
Drive** on the opening browser page, using your usual Google account, at the
start and end of a reading session. While signed in, later changes back up
automatically; Google may ask you to connect again after about an hour.
Check that Sync finishes successfully before relying on the outside copy.

If Google or the internet is unavailable, installed readings still work and
unsent changes stay on this device for retry. Keep the browser data and tap
Sync again when the connection returns. A fresh device needs an outside copy
from a successful Sync; it cannot recover changes left only on the Quest.

The optional Cloudflare backup is separate Build 059 work and is not ready.
The project budget remains zero: no paid plans or automatic paid upgrades.

## If something goes wrong

If the cabin is already open in another tab, return to that tab.
Keep the cabin's browser data: unsent changes wait there. Keep reading and
retry Sync when the internet returns. A note about a delayed backup does not
mean your reading history has been erased.

For an extra copy, open the browser page's advanced tools and choose
**Export progress**; keep the downloaded file somewhere safe. **Import progress**
restores a complete copy. Test recovery in a separate browser, keeping the
original device intact.

If a problem remains, use **Save a problem report** when offered and describe
what happened. Future work on this finished app is for bug fixes only.
